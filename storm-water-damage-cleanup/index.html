Storm water damage on the South Shore.

Storm water damage in Massapequa Park, NY comes from wind-driven rain through windows, doors, roofs and walls, and sometimes from storm surge. The 24/7 line is open during storms. Keep away from broken glass, downed wires and flooded areas, and call only when it is safe to be near the damage.

Superstorm Sandy in 2012 is the benchmark for South Shore storms, and smaller storms still push rain sideways into gaps that stay dry in ordinary weather. Storm water that has crossed the ground is often Category 2 or 3, while rain through a roof is often cleaner but picks up contamination from insulation and attic dust.

Bayfront homes face tidal as well as rain water, which the Bar Harbor page explains.

Immediate steps after storm water enters.

Prioritize safety first.

  • Stay away from downed lines and wet electrical equipment. Call the utility or 911..
  • Do not step in standing water if the power is on. Shock risk..
  • Cover broken windows and roof openings. Use plastic sheeting or tarps if you can do so safely..
  • Move belongings off wet floors. Use foil under furniture legs..
  • Photograph everything. Include the storm damage and the interior..

If the water came with sewage or rose from the ground, see the flood damage page.

Storm damage and insurance.

Wind and rain damage is often covered under homeowners policies, while flood from rising water usually needs separate flood coverage. Some policies have separate windstorm deductibles. Check your policy or ask your insurer.

Keep receipts for emergency repairs and photograph the exterior damage as well as the interior.

Ask any restoration company whether its crews work to ANSI/IICRC S500, the published standard for professional water damage restoration. What to do first: stop the source if it is safe to do so, keep clear of wet electrical equipment and call the 24/7 line.
