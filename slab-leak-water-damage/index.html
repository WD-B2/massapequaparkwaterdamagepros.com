Slab leak water damage is hidden.

Slab leak water damage in Massapequa Park, NY begins with a pipe leaking beneath a concrete floor. Water rises through cracks and joints, soaks flooring and wicks into walls. Signs include warm spots on the floor, the sound of running water with fixtures off, a water meter that moves, unexplained high bills and damp carpet.

A plumber locates and repairs the leak using acoustic or thermal tools. Restoration dries the floor, walls and any damaged finishes. Both are needed, and the order depends on how much water is on the surface.

Signs of a slab leak.

Look for these.

  • Warm spot on the floor. A hot line may be leaking..
  • Running water sound. Heard with all fixtures off..
  • Meter moving. Check with everything off..
  • Damp carpet or flooring. Especially along a line..
  • Musty smell. Moisture trapped under flooring..

How a slab leak is found and what restoration does.

Plumbers locate slab leaks with acoustic listening devices, thermal cameras or pressure tests, and then choose between repairing the line in place, rerouting it through walls or replacing the section. Which option fits depends on the age and material of the pipe and the layout of the house, and that decision belongs to the plumber.

Restoration starts once the line is shut off or repaired. Water that came up through the slab soaks carpet, pad, flooring adhesive and the base of walls. Concrete itself is porous and holds moisture, so it dries slowly and needs dehumidification rather than just airflow.

What a slab leak looks like from inside the house.

Symptoms vary with the floor covering.

Under carpet.

A damp or cool patch, a musty smell and pad that never fully dries are typical. Carpet near the leak may discolor along tack strips.

Under wood or laminate.

Boards may cup, buckle or separate at seams. See hardwood floor water damage for what can be saved.

Under tile.

Cracked grout, loose tiles and a warm spot on hot-line leaks. Water may come up through grout lines.
