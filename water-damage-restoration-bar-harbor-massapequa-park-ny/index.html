How bay water differs from a leak.

Water that comes from the bay, a canal or a storm tide is not clean. It can carry sewage, fuel, silt and debris, so it is handled as contaminated water. Salt can also stay in framing and finishes after the water is gone, which is one reason the cleanup differs from a burst pipe.

A bulkhead holds soil and water back, but heavy rain and storm tides can still reach lawns, garages and lower floors. Rain can also come through windows and doors on its own in a wind-driven storm.

Storm history, kept in perspective.

Hurricane Sandy came ashore near Atlantic City on the evening of October 29, 2012, near high tide. The National Weather Service logged a surge of about 7.9 feet at Freeport, and a local report that day mentions a high water rescue in Massapequa. These are South Shore records, not village records.

Safe first steps with bay or storm water.

Use these while you wait for help. Do not wade through deep water to save belongings.

  • Keep out of any room where water meets electricity. Do not reach for a wet panel, outlet or cord, and do not switch breakers while your feet are wet..
  • Treat bay and canal water as contaminated. Keep children and pets out, and wear waterproof boots and gloves if you must enter..
  • Do not clean up sewage yourself. A toilet or floor drain that backed up needs professional handling..
  • Keep fuel and chemical containers upright and away from water. Do not carry them through deep water..
  • Photograph the waterline, then call. Pictures of the water level and the damage help the crew and your insurer..

Insurance for waterfront homes.

Flood, meaning water rising from outside, is usually separate from a standard homeowners policy. Wind-driven rain, burst pipes and sewer backup can each be handled differently. Flood policies commonly have a waiting period, so ask your agent early, before a storm is in the forecast.

The crew provides photos, moisture readings and a written scope of work. Only your insurer decides what is covered, and no outcome can be promised here.
