Insurance questions for waterfront homes.

Owners near the bay should check two things before the next storm. The first is whether the address is in a mapped flood zone, which you can look up on the FEMA flood map. The second is whether the homeowners policy includes water backup coverage, because sewer and sump failures are often excluded unless added.

Flood insurance is separate from a standard policy and often has a waiting period. Ask your agent early. The crew can supply photos, moisture readings and a written scope after a loss, but only your insurer decides coverage.
