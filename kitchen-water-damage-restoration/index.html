Kitchen water damage hides under and behind things.

Kitchen water damage in Massapequa Park, NY often begins under the sink, behind the dishwasher or beneath the refrigerator, where leaks can run for weeks before a stain appears. If water is running now, close the shutoff valve under the sink or behind the appliance and call the 24/7 line.

Kitchens combine plumbing, appliances, cabinetry and flooring in a small space. Water spreads under cabinet bases and into subfloors, and particleboard cabinets swell when wet. Fast drying can save solid cabinets, while swollen particleboard is usually replaced.

Check these places for a leak.

Common sources in a kitchen.

  • Under the sink. Look at supply lines, the trap and the disposal..
  • Behind the dishwasher. A loose hose or door gasket can leak slowly..
  • Behind the refrigerator. Ice maker lines are a frequent culprit..
  • Around the faucet and backsplash. Failing caulk lets water behind..
  • Under the floor. Soft spots or cupping suggest long-term leaks..

Cabinets, countertops and toe kicks.

Water under a kitchen sink usually reaches the cabinet floor first, then the toe kick and then the subfloor beneath. Cabinets built from particleboard swell and lose their strength when wet, while plywood and solid wood handle water better. Stone and solid-surface counters are rarely harmed by water from below, but the cabinet boxes holding them up can be.

Crews remove toe kicks and drill small holes in cabinet bases so air can move through. If the cabinet floor is swollen, a repair contractor can replace the base or the whole run. Ask for a moisture reading of the subfloor before new cabinets go in.

How kitchen drying works.

Hidden water needs targeted drying.

  1. 1Stop the leak.Shut off the supply to the fixture or appliance..
  2. 2Extract water.Standing water is removed from the floor and cabinet bases..
  3. 3Open and inspect.Toe kicks and base panels come off so the space under cabinets can be seen..
  4. 4Dry.Air is pushed under cabinets and dehumidifiers run. See structural drying..
  5. 5Repair.Damaged cabinets, flooring and drywall are replaced by a contractor..

Kitchen leaks and insurance.

Sudden leaks from appliances or supply lines are often covered, while long-term seepage may not be. Ask your insurer how your policy treats it.
