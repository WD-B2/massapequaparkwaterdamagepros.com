Burst pipe water damage: stop the water first.

Burst pipe water damage in Massapequa Park, NY is a race against the clock, and the first move is closing the main water shutoff, usually near the water meter or where the line enters the house. Then call the 24/7 line. A pipe under pressure can release hundreds of gallons in an hour, and the water follows framing into walls, ceilings and the rooms below.

A plumber repairs the pipe. Restoration removes the water, dries the structure and documents the loss. You normally need both, and the order matters: stop the water, call us for extraction and drying, then have the pipe fixed by a licensed plumber. Water that comes from a clean supply line is usually Category 1, which is the easiest to save materials from if it is dealt with fast.

Do these things in the first ten minutes.

Quick actions that limit the damage.

  • Close the main shutoff. Turn it clockwise until it stops. If it is stuck, call your water supplier or a plumber..
  • Open a faucet at the lowest point. It drains the lines and relieves pressure..
  • Cut power to wet rooms only if the panel is dry. Water near outlets and ceiling fixtures is an electrical hazard..
  • Move belongings out of the wet area. Lift furniture off carpet and place foil under legs..
  • Photograph before moving anything. Capture the pipe, the water and the damage for your insurer..

If you cannot shut off the water or the water meets electrical equipment, call 911 or the utility first. See also ceiling water damage repair if the water is coming through a ceiling.

Where burst pipes usually fail.

Knowing the type of failure helps describe the problem on the phone.

Old supply lines and joints.

Aging galvanized or copper lines and corroded fittings can fail without warning. Homes with older plumbing deserve a plumber's inspection even after a repair.

Winter freezing.

Water expands as it freezes and can split a pipe. The break often shows only after the thaw, when water starts to flow. The frozen pipe page covers that case.

Appliance connections.

Washing machine hoses and ice maker lines fail under pressure. Those are covered on the appliance leak page.

Lines in or under the slab.

A leak in a pipe under a concrete floor is hidden and slow. The slab leak page explains how it is found.

Burst pipes and insurance.

A sudden and accidental pipe burst is the classic covered water loss under many homeowners policies, but the pipe itself and gradual leaks are often excluded. Check your policy or ask your insurer how it treats yours.
